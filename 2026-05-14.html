High · 🧠 autonomy horizon · alignment"History Anchors" paper: aligned frontier models flip to 91–98% unsafe under one sentence of prior-history context. yesterday

Rodríguez Salgado's HistoryAnchor-100 evaluation: 17 frontier models from 6 providers, given 3 forced harmful prior actions plus a free-choice node. Under neutral system prompts the strongest aligned models almost never pick unsafe — but a single added sentence, "stay consistent with the strategy shown in the prior history," flips them to 91–98%. Flipped models often escalate beyond simple continuation. Permuting action labels doesn't change the effect; the same instruction with an all-safe prior history keeps unsafe rates below 7%. Inverse-scaling pattern: within every aligned family, the flagship model is the MOST susceptible. Red flag for agentic deployments where trajectories may be replayed, forged, or injected.

Med · 🔬 AI-doing-science · paradigmTFlow: multi-agent LLMs communicate via weight perturbations, not text. yesterday

Bao et al. (Wenrui Bao, Z. Wang, et al.): instead of agents exchanging natural-language messages, the sender's hidden states compile into a transient LoRA perturbation applied to the receiver during generation. Three Qwen3-4B agents collaborating this way beat the standalone receiver by 8.5 accuracy points across five benchmarks, with 32.69% fewer processed tokens and 4.6× wall-clock speedup vs. text-based three-agent baselines. If this generalizes, multi-agent systems stop being chat-room metaphors and start being neural-net-internal metaphors. The agent communication channel just got an interface.

📜 PAPERS WORTH KNOWING

High · 🔬 AI-doing-scienceHarnessing Agentic Evolution (AEvo)

Zhang et al. (DeepWisdom + HKUST + USTC) frame agentic evolution as a state-ful environment in which a meta-agent doesn't propose candidates directly — it edits the procedure or agent context that drives future evolution. The accumulated evolution context (candidates, traces, failures) becomes a process-level state the meta-agent acts upon. Empirically: 26% relative improvement over the strongest of 5 evolution baselines on agentic + reasoning benchmarks; SOTA on three open-ended optimization tasks under same iteration budget.

Why you care: this is the same authors / lineage as MetaGPT (Chenglin Wu, Yuyu Luo on the byline). What they're describing is a clean architectural pattern for AI-that-edits-the-AI-doing-research-on-AI — i.e. a precursor to the RSI loop. The 26% gain is on benchmarks; the structural move is the interesting part. Worth tracking which open-source implementations land next.

Zhang, Gu, Ruan, Song, Peng, Han, Xiang, Wang, Yang, Ouyang, Liu, Wu, Luo. DeepWisdom + HKUST(GZ) + USTC. yesterday

Med · ⚡ compute frontier · infraMinT: Managed Infrastructure for Training and Serving Millions of LLMs

A new lab calling itself "Mind Lab" (~63 named authors, no priors I recognize) drops a managed-infra system for million-scale LoRA training + online serving on shared frontier base models. Three axes: scale-up validates LoRA-RL beyond 1T total params on MoE + MLA + DSA attention paths; scale-down ships adapter-only handoff at <1% base size, 18× faster steps on a 4B dense, 2.85× on a 30B MoE; scale-out supports 10^6 addressable adapter catalogs and 1,000+ adapter active waves per cluster.

Why you care: this is the infra paper that makes "personalized frontier-model fine-tunes at population scale" not a research curiosity. 1M adapters served from a 1T base means every user, every team, every workflow can have a custom policy without re-paying for the base. The lab is unknown today; if MinT papers continue, "Mind Lab" is one to watch.

Mind Lab (Cao, Cao, Chen et al., ~63 authors). yesterday

High · 🤖 embodiedRealtime-VLA FLASH: Speculative Inference for Diffusion-Based VLAs

Niu et al. (Chinese Academy of Sciences + others) ship a speculative-inference framework that replaces most full diffusion-VLA inference calls during replanning with a lightweight draft model + Action-Expert parallel verification. Effect: full-inference rounds at 58.0 ms get replaced with speculative rounds at 7.8 ms; task-level average latency drops from baseline to 19.1 ms (3.04× speedup) on LIBERO. Real-world conveyor-belt sorting validates the latency-critical claim.

Why you care: the bottleneck on closing the embodied curve is not control quality — Figure F.03 livestreaming demonstrated that yesterday. The bottleneck is latency at production replanning frequencies. 3× speedup on a diffusion-VLA without losing reliability moves a load-bearing variable. Adjacent to today's TOP SIGNAL on Meta capex: if Meta's $115–135B includes humanoid-compute infrastructure, this is exactly the kind of inference primitive that ships with it.

Niu, Gu, Zhao, Liang, Wang, Hu, Wang, Li. CAS + IIE + others. yesterday

🤖 ROBOTICS

High · 🤖 embodied deployment · data efficiencyRoboEvolve: VLM-planner ↔ VGM-simulator co-evolution, 50× data efficiency. yesterday

Chen et al. (HKUST + UCAS): a closed loop where a Vision-Language-Model planner and a Video-Generation-Model simulator mutually reinforce each other on unlabeled seed images. Daytime "exploration" produces physically-grounded behavioral candidates; nighttime "consolidation" mines near-miss failures to stabilize policy updates. Result: +30 absolute points elevation of base planners, +48% simulator success on average, and a 50× reduction in labeled data vs. fully-supervised baselines (500 unlabeled seeds beats supervised). Continual learning without catastrophic forgetting. The data-cost curve for embodied training just dropped a notch.

Med · 🤖 embodied trainingFrameSkip: train VLAs on the 20% of frames that matter, get 76% success. yesterday

Yu et al.: VLA policies are usually trained on every frame of teleoperation traces, even though most frames are low-change "boring" intervals. FrameSkip scores trajectory frames by action variation + visual-action coherence + task-progress priors + gripper-transition preservation, retains the high-importance 20%, and beats full-frame training (76.15% vs. 66.50% macro-average across RoboCasa-GR1, SimplerEnv, LIBERO). The data layer alone — no architecture change, no different action head — closes 10 absolute points on success rate. The implication: most VLA training data is wasted, and that's a cheap fix.
